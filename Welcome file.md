# Tech meets beauty: IBM & L’Oréal’s partnership for sustainable cosmetics

What do a tech company and a beauty brand have in common? A shared mission to make the world, and the beauty industry, more sustainable. L’Oréal, one of the biggest names in cosmetics, teamed up with IBM to use artificial intelligence (AI) in a whole new way. Together, they’re changing how makeup and skincare products are made; faster, smarter, and better for the planet. From personalized formulas to eco-friendly production, this partnership is showing how science and tech can power the future of beauty.

## 

Why sustainability matters in beauty

Sustainability is becoming a big focus in the beauty industry as people care more about protecting the planet.  **Sustainability** is the long-term ambition for people to coexist on Earth without depleting its natural resources. The goal is to create a more sustainable future for both people and the planet. By using eco-friendly practices, beauty brands can make products with ingredients that are responsibly sourced and create less waste. This helps save natural resources and meets the growing demand for products that are better for the environment.

Making beauty products in a sustainable way is important because it helps reduce pollution and protects ecosystems that are vital to life on Earth. It also encourages new ideas, like creating packaging that breaks down naturally or using materials that can be renewed. These changes not only make the products more appealing but also show a commitment to taking care of the Earth for future generations. As the beauty world changes, sustainability is leading the way in building trust and making a positive impact.

## 

IBM's role in beauty technology

IBM's advanced AI technology is driving a new era of innovation in the beauty industry. By collaborating with L’Oréal, they are creating sustainable and personalized cosmetic solutions that cater to varied needs. This partnership highlights the potential of technology to merge social responsibility with eco-conscious practices, paving the way for a more responsible and innovative future in beauty.

-   •
    
    **AI-powered cosmetic formulations:**  IBM's generative AI technology accelerates the development of innovative cosmetic formulas, enhancing efficiency and creativity in product creation.
    
-   •
    
    **Sustainability through technology:**  IBM collaborates with L'Oréal to prioritize renewable and sustainable raw materials, reducing energy and material waste in cosmetic production.
    
-   •
    
    **Personalization in beauty products:**  By leveraging AI, IBM enables L'Oréal to create personalized beauty solutions tailored to various consumer needs and preferences worldwide.
    
-   •
    
    **Revolutionizing ingredient discovery:**  IBM's AI foundation model aids in understanding renewable ingredients, transforming the formulation process for more sustainable and broad spectrum cosmetics.
