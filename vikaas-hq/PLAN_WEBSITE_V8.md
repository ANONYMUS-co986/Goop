centre (kabadiwala + authorised recycler, recruited by us) comes to your door →
weighs → pays cash → receipts it to an HSPCB-verified recycler. Households,
kabadiwalas, recyclers, cities — ONE network, ONE app.

receipts · works where others aren't — your door.

## 2. THE CRAFT BAR (user-asked)
- **Self-made 3D models** (Three.js): the app phone (rounded-box body, notch, camera,
  orbiting e-waste chips) — built from primitives, not downloaded assets.

- **Info cards**: every feature/flow/tap as a glass card with spotlight + 3D tilt +
  stamps. Mouse-reactive everything (already live: magnetic buttons, velocity skew).
