RULE 01 — WEIGH FIRST

Any number we can physically measure, we physically measure — then photograph the scale. 1.4 kg exists because a photo of it exists.

RULE 02 — SOURCE OR STAMP

Big numbers come with their home attached (govt lists, mission briefs). Estimates keep their tilde. Saying "about" is not weakness — it's the brand.

RULE 03 — COMEDY, LABELLED

Drop-dead funny, never drop-dead wrong. Every dramatised quote carries its label, on-screen, in-caption. The dare is the point.
