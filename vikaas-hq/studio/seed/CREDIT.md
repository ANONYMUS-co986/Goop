## Treatment
Music only — no sound-effect layer. Warm/contemplative (spectral centroid ~752Hz),
steady pulse, sits low and even at about -21 dB so the on-screen text leads.
Gentle 2.2s fade in, 3s fade out. Normalised to -19 LUFS (quieter than the
platform default on purpose, so it stays background).
