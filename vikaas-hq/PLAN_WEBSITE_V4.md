## 0. THE IDEA — in one paragraph (the pitch we lead with)

**Every Indian home has a drawer of dead electronics. The recyclers exist —
government-authorised, licensed, waiting. What's missing is the doorstep.**
VIKAAS (विकास) is the door-to-door e-waste flow that closes the gap: open the
drawer → weigh it (kitchen scale, real) → know what it's worth (₹40, real) →
hand it to the network (kabadi + HSPCB recyclers, real) → get a receipt
(stamped, real). The infrastructure isn't missing. **The doorstep is.** And we
proved it: 1.4 kg weighed, ₹40 earned, 15 recyclers found, 0 doorsteps served,
10 homes surveyed — every number real, every claim stamped. Mission:
**NO DRAWER LEFT BEHIND.** From one drawer in Gurugram to the 1M1B Impact
Summit at the United Nations — 20 Nov 2026.
