The whole story in 60 seconds

Everything a judge — or a parent — needs to know about Project Verde, compressed onto one page. Details wait patiently in the chapters that follow.

City plants die quietly — of forgetfulness and guesswork. Project Verde is a three-tier IoT system that lets a plant say what it needs and then acts on it: sensors on an ESP32 read the soil, air, light and water tank every second, a Firebase cloud keeps the truth, and a web app turns it into a dashboard, a weather brain and an AI plant doctor.

₹1,890Total build · ≈ $23

Full working product for a quarter of a commercial kit's price. Software & APIs: free tiers only.

5 + 25 sensors · 2 actuators

Soil, air, light, tank — watched 24/7. A pump that waters and a UV grow light that glows.

1 secCloud heartbeat

Ten metrics upstream, nine commands downstream — every single second, without a hiccup.
