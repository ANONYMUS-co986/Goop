09 · Cloud & the appWEATHER BRAIN

The sky gets a vote

Watering before a downpour is how roots drown. So the app asks Delhi's sky every three minutes — and can overrule the pump.

EVERY 3 MINUTES

The app looks up

An OpenWeatherMap call fetches Delhi's live condition + 5-day forecast: GET /data/2.5/weather?q=Delhi, key in the URL.

CONDITION DECODER

Rain codes translation

Weather IDs in the 2xx / 3xx / 5xx / 6xx families mean drizzle, rain, storm or snow. One flag becomes truth: rain_expected.

COUNTDOWN

The override arms visibly

A countdown timer shows on the Weather page. When it hits zero, the app writes weather_override = 1 to the cloud.

THE VETO

Pump barred, politely

The ESP32's AUTO logic sees the flag and holds off — even with dry soil, the rain veto wins (page 12). When the sky clears, the veto retracts.
