02 · The whyPROBLEM

Plants don't die of thirst.
They die of silence.

Nobody kills their plant on purpose. They kill it with a busy week — because a plant has no voice, no notification, no way to say "I'm dry," "you're drowning me," or "the tank is empty."

Forgotten

Urban families travel, study late, work shifts. Watering slips a day, then a week. The soil says nothing — it just quietly hits zero.

Drowned

The opposite sin: daily "just in case" watering. Roots rot in soggy soil while the plant looks, from above, perfectly loved.

Blind

No one knows the soil moisture right now, whether the tank is empty, or that rain is coming tonight. Plants die of missing information.

03 · Our answerTHE LOOP

So we built a plant that speaks up.

Verde closes the loop a human keeps forgetting to close. Four verbs, running every second of every day — for roughly the cost of four movie tickets.

1 · Sense

Five probes read soil, air, light & tank — filtered, averaged, honest.

2 · Decide

The ESP32 brain applies AUTO logic with thresholds you tune live.

3 · Act

Pump waters until the soil says "enough" — then stops, exactly there.

4 · Explain

Dashboard, weather veto, camera, AI doctor. The plant finally has a voice.

TIER 1 · EDGE

ESP32 WROOM-32 + five sensors, relay-driven pump, UV grow LED. ESP32-CAM takes the photos. No cloud needed to keep the plant alive — AUTO runs on-device.

TIER 2 · CLOUD

Firebase Realtime Database: the single source of truth. Sensors, controls, scans, weather and logs — one JSON tree, public-read, validated writes.

TIER 3 · EXPERIENCE

A single-file web app — dashboard, weather, plant doctor, AI assistants — talking to four external APIs on free tiers.
