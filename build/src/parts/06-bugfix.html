07 · The crime sceneBUG STORY

The bug that nearly sank us

AUTO mode was supposed to water until the soil hit 35%. Instead, the pump clicked on and off every ~10 seconds, like it had hiccups. This is the honest account of why.

THE SYMPTOM

tap. tap. tap.

In AUTO, the pump ran in ~10-second bursts instead of one continuous drink. The relay sounded like a telegraph key. Moisture climbed in zig-zags, not a slope.

THE FALSE LEADS

We blamed hardware. Of course we did.

Loose relay? Reseated. Power sag? Added the capacitor — it helped the rails, not the rhythm. Threshold noise? The filters were already clean. The stutter survived everything.

THE CLUE

The whole chip was rebooting, not the relay

We timestamped the serial log and saw it: a watchdog reboot every ~8–10 seconds, right in the pump's rhythm. The firmware wasn't deciding to stop — it was dying mid-decision and waking up in a different state.

THE ROOT CAUSE

17 blocking HTTPS calls per second

One Firebase call per sensor value, per control key, blocking, every loop. The network stalled, the loop starved, the 8-second watchdog fired, the chip rebooted, AUTO re-decided from scratch. On. Off. On. Off. tap. tap. tap.

08 · The fixBEFORE / AFTER

JSON bundling: the ~85% latency diet

Stop making one call per value. Pack everything into two envelopes — one up, one down. The fix took an evening; the lesson is permanent.

BEFORE · 17 CALLS / SECOND
17
  • One blocking HTTPS call per metric & per key
  • Network stalls starve the loop
  • Watchdog reboots all day
  • Pump: ~10-second stutter
AFTER · 2 CALLS / SECOND
  • 1 PUT — all 10 metrics to /sensors
  • 1 GET — all 9 keys from /controls
  • Latency down ~85%, zero watchdog reboots
  • Pump: ON until threshold, then off. Once.
// every second, exactly two envelopes:
PUT /sensors.json  ← { moisture, temperature, humidity, light, tank_level,
            lux, watchdog_status, voltage_sag, successful_uploads, failed_uploads }
GET /controls.json → { manual_mode, pump_state, light_manual_mode, grow_light_state,
            capture_photo, moisture_threshold, tank_threshold, light_threshold, weather_override }

What it taught us

Count your network calls like coins. A microcontroller doesn't grief you for bad logic — it griefs you for chattering. Two fat envelopes beat seventeen thin letters.
