04 · How it worksRHYTHM

The one-second heartbeat

Every second, forever, the same disciplined routine. No delay(), no blocking calls, no excuses — a millis() scheduler keeps every task in its lane.

1 s

Sensors + cloud

Fresh metrics out, fresh commands in. The dashboard never sits on stale data.

1.5 s

CAM polls

The camera asks "photo requested?" — after the main brain, never blocking it.

10 s

WiFi health

Drop? Reconnect. Falls back across home → hotspot → school networks.

60 s

History logging

A moisture point lands in /historical_logs every minute — fuel for the trend chart and tomorrow's predictive watering.

3 min

Weather check

The app re-reads Delhi's sky. Rain brewing? A countdown starts, then the pump is vetoed. Page 17.

Why the obsession with "non-blocking"?

Because the one time we let network calls block the loop, the watchdog ate us — a reboot every 10 seconds and a pump that stuttered like a telegraph key. That story gets its own spread: page 15.
