The bench — artwork of our actual layout

ESP32 WROOM-32 — the brain

32-bit · WiFi

Runs AUTO logic, filters, thresholds and the 1-second heart­beat. Keeps watering even if WiFi dies.

ESP32-CAM — the eyes

OV2640

Shoots SVGA plant portraits on demand, uploads within seconds. Own board, own firmware, zero load on the brain.

Soil moisture

AO → GPIO 34 VCC gate → GPIO 23

LM393 comparator, read for just 15 ms at a time — power-gating stops probe corrosion.

DHT11

DATA → GPIO 4

Air temperature + humidity. Cheap, slow, honest — sampled once a second.

LDR module

AO → GPIO 35

Sees dusk arrive. "Dark" triggers the grow light; ±2% hysteresis stops flicker at sunset.

HC-SR04

TRIG → 18 ECHO → 19

Ultrasonic tank level. 5-point filter rejects pump-splash garbage — a splash can't fake an empty tank.

2-ch relay → pump

IN1 → GPIO 5 ACTIVE-LOW

Switches the 5 V submersible pump on its own isolated supply. The brain never touches pump current.

UV grow LED

GPIO 12 ACTIVE-HIGH · 220 Ω

Photosynthetic light on schedule or on command — sunrise on demand for a windowsill plant.

Why two microcontrollers?

The camera is a diva: it wants RAM, clean power and precise timing. Give it its own board and the brain stays fast — one waters, one watches, neither waits for the other.
